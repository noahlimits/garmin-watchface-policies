# OmaFork privacy policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026  
Developer: BetterLiving  
Contact: committed@gmail.com

OmaFork is a Garmin Connect IQ watch face. It reads time, date, watch battery, heart-rate and Body Battery history, activity metrics, altitude, Garmin Weather, and available location information to display the watch face and calculate sunrise and sunset.

OmaFork saves the last valid location in its local app storage on the watch so it can calculate sunrise and sunset when a current position is unavailable. It also saves the display preferences you choose. These data are used only to operate the watch face.

OmaFork has no developer-operated server, account, advertising, or analytics. Its code does not send your location, sensor readings, or settings to BetterLiving. Garmin's device, Connect IQ Store, and weather services operate under Garmin's own privacy practices.

The locally cached location is replaced when a newer valid location becomes available and otherwise remains in OmaFork's app storage while the app is installed. You can stop the watch face's access to these data by removing it from your device. For questions, contact committed@gmail.com.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
