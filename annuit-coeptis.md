# Annuit Cœptis privacy policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026  
Developer: BetterLiving  
Contact: committed@gmail.com

Annuit Cœptis is a Garmin Connect IQ watch face. It reads time, date, battery level, Garmin Weather conditions, recent heart-rate and elevation history, and location information available from the watch to display the watch face and calculate sunrise and sunset times.

The watch face saves the last valid location in its local storage on the watch so it can calculate sunrise and sunset when a current position is unavailable. It also saves the display preferences you choose through Garmin's app settings. These data are used only for the watch face's functions.

Annuit Cœptis has no developer-operated server, account, advertising, or analytics. Its code does not send your location, sensor readings, or settings to BetterLiving or other third parties. Garmin's device, Connect IQ Store, and weather services operate under Garmin's own privacy practices.

The locally cached location is replaced when a newer valid location becomes available and otherwise remains in the watch face's app storage while the app is installed. You can stop the watch face's access to these data by removing it from your device. For questions about this policy or the watch face, contact committed@gmail.com.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
